Privacy Policy & Child Safety

Effective Date: 31 October 2026

1. Privacy by Design for Young Learners

Little Explorers is built for young children aged 3–5 years and their families. We strictly adhere to child data privacy best practices and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023).

2. Minimal Information Collected

  • Child profiles only require an optional nickname and learning level (Nursery/LKG).
  • We do NOT require or collect full legal names, exact birth dates, photographs, or GPS locations.
  • We do NOT record or store children's voices.

3. Household Data Ownership

Questions, custom activities, and learning progress created by parents are stored locally in the browser's IndexedDB storage and remain private to the parent's household by default. Parents retain full rights to edit, export (JSON), or delete all records at any time.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising

Little Explorers is 100% free of child-directed advertising, behavioural trackers, and commercial profiling.

5. Screen-Time & Healthy Development

In accordance with early childhood health guidance, we design short, self-contained educational activities that discourage excessive sedentary screen time. We actively encourage physical offline activities with parents.
